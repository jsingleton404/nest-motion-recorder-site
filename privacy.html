Privacy policy

Nest Motion Recorder · Effective November 18, 2026

Nest Motion Recorder is a personal, non-commercial app that John Singleton runs on his own PC for his own Google Nest cameras. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the camera owner's permission, through Google's Smart Device Management API, it reads the names of the authorized cameras, receives their motion and person event notifications, and opens a live video stream when an event occurs. Audio from the stream is discarded and never saved.

How the data is used and stored

Video clips are saved as MP4 files on the owner's PC and copied to the owner's home network storage. A log of events and recording results is kept on the same PC. The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on that PC. Nothing is uploaded to any other service, sold, shared or used for advertising, and the app has no analytics.

Retention and deletion

The owner decides how long clips are kept and can delete them at any time. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data

Nest Motion Recorder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
